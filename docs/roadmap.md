# Roadmap: open issues and plans

This is the single list of everything still open in the backend: known bugs and risks, follow-up work, decisions to keep things as they are, and what became of the earlier design plans. It replaces the old `docs/backlog.md` and the plan/spec files that were in `docs/superpowers/`. The outcome of each superpowers plan is summarized in [Earlier plans and what became of them](#earlier-plans-and-what-became-of-them); their full text is in git history before this commit.

Fixed items are described in [`fixed-issues.md`](fixed-issues.md). Deploy steps are in [`operations.md`](operations.md#deploying-an-upgrade).

Status as of 15 November 2026, checked against the code on branch `chore/local-dev-fixes`.

## Earlier plans and what became of them

All six were written on 12 November 2026.

### Plan: Messaging outbox/inbox

- **Goal:** every RabbitMQ event goes through a transactional outbox with a relay; every consumer deduplicates through an inbox and dead-letters failures. Rollout step 2 of the hybrid design.
- **Status: done**, and extended beyond the plan.
- **Exists now:** `libs/messaging` (`OutboxPublisher`, `OutboxRelay` with back-off, `mandatory` publish and cleanup, `InboxService`, `defineConsumerQueue` with 5 retries 1-16 s then `<q>.dlq`); every service with a database runs the relay; the Messaging dashboard, `OutboxStuck` and `DeadLetters` alerts, and RabbitMQ scrape (`docker/observability/`). The legacy bare-payload path is removed (`libs/messaging/src/lib/envelope.ts` throws).
- **Left:** nothing. `exam.writing.submitted` stays bare on purpose (external scorer).

### Plan: Observability and health

- **Goal:** OpenTelemetry from every app to a local Grafana LGTM stack, `/health/live` and `/health/ready` everywhere, a Platform status dashboard and alerts. Rollout step 1 of the hybrid design.
- **Status: done.**
- **Exists now:** `libs/telemetry` (`startTelemetry`, `HealthModule` with readiness registry and `service_ready`), all 10 apps on the `main.ts` → `bootstrap.ts` split with `HealthModule`, `docker/docker-compose.observability.yaml`, `docker/observability/dashboards/{platform-status,messaging}.json`, `alerting/platform.yaml` (`ServiceNotReady` plus one `ServiceMissing` per app), compose healthchecks, `docs/operations.md`.
- **Left:** only the hybrid-specific parts: the "Authz sync health" dashboard and the "authz catch-up done" readiness check, which depend on the unbuilt snapshot sync.

### Plan: Backlog goal

- **Goal:** fix the S- and C-series audit items, move JWTs to HttpOnly cookies, add limited exponential-backoff retries to consumers, write the Restate design, update the frontend.
- **Status: done.**
- **Exists now:** every task shipped and was deployed (S2-S12, S14, C1-C16, cookie sessions with `CookieIssuanceGuard`, `<q>.retry.N` queues, the frontend changes). Later rounds also closed S16-S25 and C20-C40 (see [`fixed-issues.md`](fixed-issues.md)). S1, S13 and C10 were closed by decision.
- **Left:** nothing from the plan. The follow-ups in this file came after it.

### Plan: Actor + permission authorization (reports pilot)

- **Goal:** the gateway forwards the caller's JWT in gRPC metadata; a shared `ActorAuthGuard` verifies it in each service and exposes `Actor { id, permissions }`; reports are decided by a domain policy on the permission `resource:report:manage` (granted to Admin and Mod by a seeded catalogue in auth). Then flash cards, notifications, exam and live would follow, `requesterId`/`privileged`, `@ResourceAccess` and `@HasRoles` would be retired, and the authorization service's ownership store removed.
- **Status: not started.** No `Actor`, `ActorAuthGuard`, `@CurrentActor` or `resource:report:manage` exists in `apps/` or `libs/`; the gateway does not forward the token; no service except live (for sockets) verifies JWTs.
- **What was done instead:** the **requester pattern**. The gateway puts `requesterId` + `privileged` (from `requesterOf(req.user, roles)`) into the request and the owning service decides from its own rows. It covers reports, flash cards and lists, exam management, live moderation and file attaches. Gateway `@HasRoles` and the shared `Permission` enum in `@server/typing` are still the norm.
- **Left:** tasks 1-3 (Actor, guard, token forwarding) are the fix for the "services trust identity fields" issue above. Tasks 4-7 (permission catalogue, per-service policies) are optional on top.

### Spec: Hybrid authorization with synced local snapshots

- **What it proposed:**
  - The authorization service becomes the single source of truth for who owns what, stored as relationship tuples (`objectType, objectId, relation, subjectType, subjectId`; only `owner` for now) with a versioned, append-only change log.
  - **Every service keeps a local copy** (`authz_tuple` + `authz_sync_state`) of the tuples for its own object types, and **pulls changes** from authorization by version (`PullChanges`, `ExportSnapshot` for bootstrap), triggered by RabbitMQ nudges (`authz.changed.<type>`), a 30 s safety poll, and a catch-up before the service reports ready.
  - Everyday operations are authorized **locally** from that snapshot plus the JWT's permissions, and logged to a local `audit_log`. A `Check` RPC is the fallback.
  - **Deleting a top-level object** (blog, list, report, exam, room) goes gateway → authorization `DeleteResource`: two-phase (`pending_delete` locks the object, the owning service applies the delete from an event and confirms, compensation on failure), 204 within 5 s or 202 + operation id.
  - Creation registers ownership via `authz.ownership.register` through the outbox, with a provisional local tuple. Sync metrics, an "Authz sync health" dashboard and alerts.
  - Rollout: (1) observability, (2) outbox/inbox, (3) tuples + sync API, (4) resource pilot, (5) exam and live, (6) remove legacy paths.
- **Status: partly; only the shared groundwork exists.** Steps 1 and 2 are done (the two plans above). Steps 3-6 are **not started**:
  - No service keeps a local copy of authorization data. There is no `authz_tuple`, `authz_sync_state`, `relation_tuple`, `change_log`, `sensitive_operation` or `audit_log` table anywhere.
  - `proto/authorization.proto` has a single RPC, `CheckOwnership`. No `PullChanges`, `ExportSnapshot`, `Check`, `DeleteResource` or `GetOperation`.
  - The authorization service is still the old `resource_ownership` table (`apps/authorization/prisma/schema/ownership.prisma`), fed by `resource.resource.*`, `exam.exam.*` and `live.room.*` events and read only by `@ResourceAccess` on blog update/delete and room delete/schedule.
  - Deletes go straight to the owning service. Nothing is routed through authorization.
- **What the code does today** is close to the opposite direction: authorization moved **into** the owning services, which decide from their own columns (`blog.authorId`, `flashCardList.authorId`, `report.reportedBy`, `exam.createdBy`, `Room.createdBy`) with no copy and no sync, given who is asking by the gateway (requester pattern). The authorization service is shrinking (unused RPCs were removed; exam and flash card ownership rows are written but no longer read).
- **Left:** a decision. Either drop the design (finish the requester migration for blogs and rooms, then remove the authorization service) or approve it as a new plan starting at step 3. The main problems it set out to solve are smaller now: events are no longer lost (outbox, retries, DLQ), and most ownership checks don't need a network hop.

### Spec: Restate adoption

- **What it proposed:** adopt Restate (a durable execution engine) for multi-step workflows only, not repo-wide. Pilot on exam attempt scoring (W1), then the hybrid design's sensitive-delete saga (W2). Keep outbox + RabbitMQ for events and BullMQ for sweeps. Seven decision points (D1-D7) were left to the owner.
- **Status: not started; no decision recorded.** No Restate code, dependency or compose service exists (`grep -ri restate` over `apps`, `libs`, `docker`, `package.json` finds nothing).
- **What changed since:** both problems that justified the W1 pilot were fixed without Restate. Scoring is now durable (exam consumes its own `exam.attempt.submitted` through the inbox, with retries and DLQ), and writing points reach the badges through `exam.attempt.rescored` (see `fixed-issues.md`, "Exam scoring was not durable"). W2 depends on the unbuilt hybrid delete saga.
- **Left:** nothing concrete. Revisit only if a real multi-step flow with long waits or compensation appears.

## How to add an item

Add new problems or plans to the matching section of this file, with the file(s) involved, the impact and a suggested fix. Confirm a suspected bug against the code first, or mark it "not verified". When you fix an item, delete it here in the same commit and describe the fix in [`fixed-issues.md`](fixed-issues.md) (and in `operations.md` "Deploying an upgrade" if deploying it needs a step).
